About Multi Therapy

Every Individual Deserves Better Care.

Multi Therapy is a dedicated multidisciplinary healthcare platform designed to empower Cerebral Palsy patients, certified medical practitioners, licensed physiotherapists, and family caregivers with unified clinical coordination.

🎯

Our Mission

To bridge clinical healthcare and daily home rehabilitation, ensuring every child and adult with neuromuscular conditions receives compassionate, data-driven, and timely therapy.

🔬

Evidence-Based Therapy

We integrate physical therapy, occupational routines, speech training, and assistive technology regimens tailored specifically to each individual's mobility assessments.

🤝

Connected Care Network

Seamless coordination connecting doctors, physiotherapists, patients, and caregivers through real-time appointments, encrypted medical charts, and interactive progress milestones.

Multidisciplinary Clinical Specialties

Guided by senior specialists in pediatric neurology, rehabilitation medicine, and clinical physiotherapy.

🩺

Pediatric Neurology

Clinical diagnosis, spasticity management, and treatment plans.

🏃

Physical Therapy

Motor control exercises, posture support, and gait rehabilitation.

🧩

Occupational Therapy

Adaptive daily living skills, sensory integration, and fine motor tasks.

🗣️

Speech & Communication

Swallowing therapy, phonation, and augmentative communication systems.
